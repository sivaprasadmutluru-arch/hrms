# CARR predefined job titles and roles

These defaults are available by department in CARR Settings and Create a CAR. Existing company job positions and their associated roles are merged into the catalog. The Create CAR form starts with Assigned To. Selecting an employee fills Job Title from their Employee-module Job Position and fills their department automatically. Matching catalog role descriptions are supplied for review. Job Title is read-only in CARR; update Job Position in the Employee module to change it.

Department aliases and combined department names are recognised. Other departments receive predefined Manager, Executive and Coordinator titles using the department name.

Company positions can be maintained in the existing organisation settings. The defaults do not change an employee's assigned designation.
